SUBMIT

Tifosæ publishes fiction, nonfiction, and poetry where sport is structural — not scenery. We want writing that couldn't exist without its sport: the relationship, the obsession, the grief, the body, the ritual has to be built through the sports content, not just touched by it in passing.

The test: if this piece would run in a traditional sports publication, it's not for us. If a single sports detail were removed and the piece would survive untouched, it's also not for us.

We're open to fandom, betting, fantasy leagues, youth sports parenting, gym culture, and other sport-adjacent obsessions — as long as sport is doing real structural work, not just supplying color.
