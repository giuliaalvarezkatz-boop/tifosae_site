TIFOSÆ

About Search Submit

TIFOSO: from the italian tifo, the fever-inducing disease typhus. It has since become a metaphor to describe the fervor afflicting sports fans.

TIFOSÆ publishes fiction, nonfiction, and poetry about sport: We want the drama, the ecstasy, the grief, the nail-chewing, the throats rubbed raw from a day spent screaming, the adulation, the rage, and everything in between.

If the piece could run in a traditional sport publication, it's not for us. If all sport details were removed and the piece would survive, it's also probably not for us. Trust your judgment but please don't make us regret it!

HOUSEKEEPING:

  • Fiction and nonfiction under 3,000 words.
  • Send up to 3 poems per submission, no more than 5 pages.
